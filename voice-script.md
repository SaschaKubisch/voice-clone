# Voice recording script

Read this text out loud to record your voice. It takes about 1.5 minutes. There are ten parts, and each part has its own mood. The small slanted line under each heading tells you how to read. Do not read that line out loud.

## 1. Calm
*Read slowly, like a bedtime story.*

Once upon a time, a little cat lived in a small house by the river. Every evening she sat by the window and watched the sun go down.

## 2. Happy
*Smile and speak a bit faster.*

Yes! We did it! This is the best day ever! Look, the cake is ready, and it looks so good. I am so happy!

## 3. Sad
*Speak slowly and softly.*

Oh no. My red balloon flew away. I held it so tight, but the wind took it. Now it is gone, and I miss it.

## 4. Surprised
*Let your voice go up at the end of each question.*

Wait, what? A frog in the kitchen? How did it get in? Who left the door open? Really? That is so funny, isn't it?

## 5. Cross
*Use short, strong words. Get louder.*

No! Stop that right now. I asked you three times. Put the toy back. I mean it. That is not okay!

## 6. Friendly
*Talk like you talk to a good friend. Laugh on "Ha".*

Hey, guess what? Um, I tried to ride my bike with no hands. Ha! I fell in the grass. But it was fun. Come with me next time!

## 7. Clear
*Speak slowly and say every number.*

Please remember this. We meet on Monday, the fifth of May, at ten past nine. Bring two apples, three pens and forty-two coins.

## 8. Tricky sounds
*Say every sound clearly.*

She sells seashells by the seashore. Red lorry, yellow lorry. Three thin thieves think. Very wet whales wave. The king sings about his treasure in June.

## 9. Quiet, then loud
*Whisper first. From "Hey!" call out loud.*

Shh, the baby is sleeping. Walk very quietly. Hey! Over here! Can you hear me? Come quick!

## 10. Calm and kind
*End in a calm, kind voice.*

Today was a good day. We played, we learned and we laughed. Thank you for listening. Good night.
