# Voice recording script

An English script for recording a voice sample, about 3 minutes long (474 spoken words). It has ten sections, one speaking style each. The italic line under each heading is a reading direction and is not read aloud. The spoken text is written exactly as it is said, numbers included, so it can serve as the transcript for the "Recording + transcript" mode.

## 1. Calm and steady
*Read slowly and evenly, like reading a story aloud.*

Let me start slowly and calmly, the way I would read a story aloud. The village sits at the edge of a wide valley, with a narrow road that winds between the orchards. In the evening the light turns golden, the wind settles, and you can hear the bell from almost three kilometres away.

## 2. Happy and excited
*Smile while you speak. Let the voice rise and speed up.*

Oh, this is wonderful news! I honestly didn't expect it at all. We actually did it! After all those weeks of work, everything finally came together, and it looks even better than I imagined. I can't stop smiling. Come here, you have to see this. It's absolutely brilliant!

## 3. Sad and quiet
*Slow down, lower the voice, leave pauses.*

I'm sorry. I really thought it would turn out differently. Sometimes you do everything right, and it still isn't enough. The house feels very quiet now. I keep looking at the empty chair by the window, and I remember how we used to sit there together, just talking, until it got dark.

## 4. Curious and surprised
*Let each question rise at the end.*

Wait, are you serious? You found it where? Under the stairs? How on earth did it get there? And who put it back, if nobody had the key? Hmm. Do you think it was there the whole time, or did someone move it last night? Really? That's strange, isn't it?

## 5. Firm and annoyed
*Short, hard sentences. Get louder towards the end.*

No. Stop right there. I've asked you three times already, and I'm not going to ask again. This is not acceptable, and you know it. If the work isn't finished by Friday, we will have a serious problem. Don't tell me it's fine. It is not fine!

## 6. Relaxed and friendly
*Talk as you would to a friend, with natural hesitations. Laugh on "Ha".*

Okay, so, here's the thing. Um, I wasn't sure at first, you know? But then I thought, why not, let's just try it. And honestly? It was fun. Ha! I mean, I nearly fell off the ladder, but still. You should come along next time. I'd really like that.

## 7. Clear and precise
*Speak like dictating a message. Say every number fully.*

Please write this down. The meeting is on Thursday, the twenty-first of November, at a quarter past nine. Bring the blue folder, the receipts from June, July and August, and four hundred and sixty-five euros. The code is seven, zero, three, eight. Ask for Doctor Elizabeth Thornton, at twelve Birch Lane.

## 8. Tricky sounds
*Keep a steady pace and pronounce every sound.*

Now some tricky sounds. She sells seashells by the seashore. Red lorry, yellow lorry. Thirty-three thieves thought they thrilled the throne throughout Thursday. Vivid violet vines wove over the wall. The king was singing while judging the measure of the azure treasure, then chose cheese, jam and orange juice.

## 9. Soft, then loud
*Start almost whispering. From "Hey!" call out across a distance.*

Shh, keep your voice down, the baby is finally asleep. Let's tiptoe past the door, very gently. Hey! Over here! Can you hear me? Bring the rope, quickly! We need help on this side!

## 10. Confident and warm
*A steady, convinced closing.*

So here is what I believe. Good work takes patience, attention and a little courage. We won't get everything right the first time, and that's fine. What matters is that we learn, adjust and keep going. Thank you for listening.
